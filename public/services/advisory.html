What it looks like

  • Regular sessions going through what the numbers actually show, not just what they say.
  • Cash flow and margin reviewed as a trend, so you can see where the business is heading.
  • Thinking through the decisions that move the needle — hiring, pricing, taking on larger jobs, expanding.
  • A second read from someone who already knows your books before you commit to something significant.

How it differs from bookkeeping

Bookkeeping tells you what happened. Advisory is about what to do next. It is an added service that creates a more involved relationship — we are in your numbers regularly and available when a decision comes up, rather than delivering statements each quarter and stepping back.

Who it is for

Owners who want more than clean books: businesses at a point where the next decision is a big one, or where growth has outpaced the owner’s visibility into the finances.

Good to know

Advisory is built on top of current, accurate books. If you are behind, cleanup and catch-up comes first — strategy built on incomplete records is guesswork.

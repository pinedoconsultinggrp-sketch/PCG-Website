A year of catch-up usually takes about three days. The real driver is transaction volume — a business running a handful of transactions a week moves faster than one running hundreds. We tell you which you are after looking at the file.

What you get

  • Bank and credit card history reconciled for the period you are behind on.
  • Miscategorized and uncategorized transactions corrected, so the totals mean something.
  • Duplicates and gaps identified and resolved.
  • Financial statements for the caught-up period, and books ready to file from.
  • A plain-language summary of what we found and where you actually stand.

How it works

  1. You give us access to the file and the accounts. We look at how far behind you are and how many transactions are involved.
  2. We quote the work before starting — you will know the cost up front.
  3. We work through the backlog and come back to you with a consolidated list of questions rather than a stream of them.
  4. You get current books, and can move onto ongoing bookkeeping so it does not happen again.

Who it is for

Owners facing a filing deadline with incomplete records, businesses that let the books slide through a busy stretch, and anyone taking over a file from a previous bookkeeper and unsure what is in it.

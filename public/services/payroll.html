Payroll

Pay runs, filings and year-end forms handled in QuickBooks Payroll, with the results flowing straight into your books rather than sitting in a separate system.

What you get

  • Pay runs processed on your schedule, whether that is weekly, biweekly or monthly.
  • Payroll tax filings and deposits made on time.
  • Year-end forms prepared and issued — W-2s for employees, 1099s for contractors.
  • Payroll entries reflected in your books, so labour cost shows up in your financial statements without a separate reconciliation.
  • Questions from your crew answered in English or Spanish.

How it works

  1. We set up or review your QuickBooks Payroll configuration — pay schedules, rates, deductions and tax registrations.
  2. You send hours, or your crew reports them, before each run.
  3. We process the run, file the associated taxes, and post the results to your books.
  4. At year end, we prepare and issue the forms.

Who it is for

Businesses with employees, contractors, or both. Payroll pairs naturally with bookkeeping — running them together is what keeps labour cost visible in your financials instead of arriving as a year-end surprise.
